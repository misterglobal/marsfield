# Marsfield Studio V2 Ethos

## Product thesis

**Creative work first. Tools second.**

Marsfield should feel like one persistent creative studio, not a collection of model forms. People begin with an idea, assemble references, generate candidates, refine promising work, and preserve the path that produced it. Models and technical controls support that flow without becoming the product's primary structure.

## Product principles

1. **Start from intent.** The primary entry point is a universal composer that accepts the outcome the creator wants, not a demand to choose a tool first.
2. **Reveal complexity in context.** Keep the workspace calm. Put controls in a contextual inspector and reveal advanced settings when they become relevant.
3. **Make assets first-class.** Images, clips, audio, characters, prompts, and references are selectable objects with identity, metadata, lineage, and reusable actions.
4. **Preserve creative continuity.** Projects remember drafts, selections, settings, references, generations, and workspace state across sessions.
5. **Show cause and effect.** Every generated asset exposes what created it: source assets, prompt, model, settings, and parent generation.
6. **Prefer direct manipulation.** Creators should select, arrange, compare, reuse, and transform assets in place instead of repeatedly navigating through forms.
7. **Support exploration without fear.** Generations are additive and recoverable. Failed attempts remain understandable, and successful branches are easy to continue.
8. **Use intelligence as guidance.** Creative Director and model intelligence may recommend, explain, and prepare actions, but the creator remains in control.
9. **Design for trust.** Loading, cost, progress, cancellation, failure, and retry behavior must be explicit. Never hide irreversible or credit-consuming actions.
10. **Work everywhere.** Desktop can provide a dense canvas and inspector; smaller screens preserve the same concepts through progressive disclosure rather than reduced capability.

## Core product model

Studio V2 is organized around four durable concepts:

- **Project:** the persistent container for a creative objective and its workspace state.
- **Asset:** a selectable creative object, whether uploaded, generated, extracted, or saved as a reusable reference.
- **Generation:** an asynchronous attempt that records intent, inputs, configuration, status, outputs, and cost.
- **Lineage:** the graph connecting source assets, generations, variants, edits, and reusable characters or references.

Tools are actions over these concepts. They should not create isolated product silos.

## Experience contract

A creator should always be able to answer:

- What am I working on?
- What is selected?
- What will happen if I generate?
- What inputs and settings are being used?
- What is running, complete, failed, or cancelled?
- Where did this asset come from?
- What can I do with it next?
- Will my work still be here when I return?

If the interface cannot answer those questions, the design is incomplete.

## Boundaries

Studio V2 does not imply a single model, media type, or linear workflow. The foundation must support image, video, audio, and future modalities while keeping provider-specific controls contained. It must preserve existing authorization, ownership, credit, safety, and provider-spend boundaries.
